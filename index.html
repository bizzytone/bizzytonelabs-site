The Sports

Everyone else is talking about the game. The Sports gives you a few current, fact-based things to say, so you can join in without being a sports expert.

  • One line you can actually say. Every talking point comes with a fact-first line to bring up.
  • The details behind it. Each card includes the players and the stats from the last game, so you know what you’re talking about.
  • Your team, your tone. Pick the sports and favorite teams you care about, and how you like to sound.
  • Sources and dates on everything. You can see where each point came from and how fresh it is.
