Privacy Policy for The Sports

Effective October 26, 2026

This policy explains what information The Sports (the “app”) handles and what we do with it. The app is published by Bizzytone Labs LLC (“Bizzytone Labs,” “we,” “us”).

The short version

  • The Sports does not ask you to create an account.
  • The app contains no advertising and no third-party analytics or tracking.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The choices you make in the app are stored on your device.

Information stored on your device

To work the way you set it up, the app saves the following on your iPhone:

  • The sports you turn on and the favorite team you pick for each.
  • Your chosen tone and your settings, including notification preferences.
  • Your Helpful and Skip ratings on talking points.

This information stays on your device. It is not sent to us, and we cannot see it. You can erase it at any time from Settings → Delete my account and data inside the app, or by deleting the app.

Information we collect

We do not collect personal information through the app. We do not ask for your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, or payment details.

If you email us, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address, and we use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

Notifications

The app includes notification preferences that are saved on your device. If we add push notifications, the app will ask for your permission first, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings. We will update this policy before that feature is released.

Third parties

We do not share information about you with third parties, because we do not collect it. Talking points are written from public news pages and box scores, and each one shows its source and date. Following a source link opens that website, which has its own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The Sports is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Your choices

You can change your sports, teams, tone and notification preferences at any time in the app’s settings, and delete your saved data as described above. If you have emailed us and want that correspondence deleted, write to us at the address below.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
