Privacy Policy for The Sports

Effective [LAUNCH DATE]

This policy explains what information The Sports (the “app”) handles and what we do with it. The app is published by Bizzytone Labs LLC (“Bizzytone Labs,” “we,” “us”).

The short version

  • The Sports does not ask you to create an account, and we never ask for your name or email address in the app.
  • The app contains no advertising, and we do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • The app sends us a small amount of anonymous usage information so we can see how many people use it and what they find helpful. You can turn this off in Settings.
  • We do not sell your personal information, and we only look at usage information as totals, never person by person.

Information stored on your device

To work the way you set it up, the app saves the following on your iPhone:

  • The sports you turn on and the favorite team you pick for each.
  • Your chosen tone and your settings.
  • Your Helpful and Skip ratings on talking points.
  • A random ID the app creates the first time you open it (see below).

You can erase all of this at any time from Settings → Delete my account and data inside the app, or by deleting the app.

Anonymous usage information we collect

When “Share anonymous usage” is on (it is on by default, and you can turn it off in Settings), the app sends us:

  • a random ID created by the app, which is not connected to your name, email address, Apple ID or any account;
  • when the app is opened;
  • which sports and teams you have selected;
  • your Helpful and Skip ratings and which tone you chose; and
  • the app version and the date and time of each of these events.

We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, photos, location, payment details, or your device’s advertising identifier. We do not store your IP address, and we do not collect anything that follows you across other companies’ apps or websites.

How we use it

  • To run and improve the app: for example, to see which sports are popular, which talking points people find helpful, and whether people come back.
  • As totals only. We look at the information in groups, such as “what share of people follow football.” We do not look at what one person did, and we do not try to work out who anyone is.
  • To share totals. We may share group totals with partners, sponsors, or people who may license or buy the app, for example “the app has this many weekly users.” These totals do not identify individuals.

We do not sell or rent individual-level information, and we do not use it for advertising.

Who handles it

We use Cloudflare, Inc. to receive and store this information on our behalf. Cloudflare may only use it to provide that service to us. We keep usage information for up to 24 months and then delete it or reduce it to totals.

Your choices

  • Turn it off: go to Settings → Share anonymous usage and switch it off. The app then stops sending usage information.
  • Delete it: Settings → Delete my account and data erases your saved choices and your random ID from your phone and asks us to delete the usage information linked to that ID. You can also email us the request, but because the ID is random we can only find your information if you can send us the ID, which appears under Settings.
  • You can change your sports, teams and tone at any time in the app’s settings.

Information you send us

If you email us, we receive the information you include in your message, such as your email address, and we use it only to reply to you and to improve the app.

Notifications

The app does not currently send notifications. If we add push notifications, the app will ask for your permission first, and you can turn them off at any time in iOS Settings. We will update this policy before that feature is released.

Third-party links

Talking points are written from public news pages and box scores, and each one shows its source and date. Following a source link opens that website, which has its own privacy practices.

Children’s privacy

The Sports is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the app handles information, we will update this page and the effective date above before the change takes effect. If the change is significant, we will also tell you in the app.
