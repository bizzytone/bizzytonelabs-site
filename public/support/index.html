Common questions

What is The Sports?

An iPhone app that gives you current, fact-based talking points so you can join sports conversations with friends and family, even if you don’t follow sports closely.

How do I change my team or sports?

Open Settings → Sports and teams. You can turn sports on or off and pick a different favorite team any time.

Where do the talking points come from?

They’re written from public news pages and box scores. Every card shows its source and the date it’s current as of.

The “Update talking points” button says nothing new or shows a limit. Is something wrong?

No. If there’s nothing new yet, the app tells you so. There’s also a daily limit on updates. Check your connection if it says you’re offline, then try again.

How do I delete my data?

Go to Settings → Delete my account and data. Your choices and ratings are stored on your device, so this erases them completely. Deleting the app does the same. See our Privacy Policy.

I spotted a mistake in a talking point.

Thank you. Email us a screenshot of the card and what looks wrong. We check every report against the source.
